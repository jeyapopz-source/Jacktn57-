# Jack TN57 (திண்டுக்கல்)

**Jack TN57** is a modern vehicle companion and roadside assistant application built for drivers, vehicle owners, and commuters in the TN-57 (Dindigul) transport district and Tamil Nadu.

## Features

- 🚗 **Vehicle & Document Locker**: Track multiple vehicles (Cars, Two-Wheelers, Autos, Commercial), registration numbers, odometer, and visual countdowns for Insurance, Pollution (PUC), and Fitness Certificate (FC) expiries.
- 🔧 **Jack & Roadside Emergency Toolkit**: Step-by-step illustrated guides for safe vehicle jacking, flat tire replacement, emergency spare tire placement, and battery jump starting.
- 🎒 **Vehicle Safety Kit Checklist**: Track 10 essential road safety tools with an interactive progress tracker before highway journeys.
- 🏛️ **TN-57 RTO Directory**: Official contact information, addresses, timings, and one-tap dialing for Dindigul Collectorate RTO, Palani Unit Office, Oddanchatram, and Batlagundu.
- 📜 **Tamil Nadu Traffic Penalties & Fines**: Motor Vehicles Act section violation reference and penalty amounts (helmet, speeding, signal jumps, mobile phone use, insurance).
- ⛽ **Fuel & Mileage Log**: Track fuel refills, fuel cost per liter, calculate real-time km/L mileage economy, and keep service logs.
- 🚦 **RTO LLR Traffic Signs Quiz**: Practice quiz for Tamil Nadu Learner's License computer tests with immediate explanation of road signs and rules.
- 🆘 **One-Tap Emergency Hotline**: Direct access to NHAI 1033 Highway Patrol, 108 Ambulance, and 100 Police.

## Tech Stack

- **Platform**: Android
- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose with Material Design 3
- **Build System**: Gradle 9.3.1 with Kotlin DSL
